# Yinling look mechanics

The feet, shorts, and torso remain front-facing and planted. The head turns at the neck with subtle natural follow-through of the long hair, while the ribbon and sleeve band stay attached in their canonical positions. The eyes remain solid black capsules, with their placement following the visible face surface during head yaw and pitch.

Up: lift the chin and face plane, expose the underside of the chin above the collar, shorten the visible forehead and shift the face upward. Down: lower the chin toward the bow, reveal more hair crown, foreshorten the face and move eyes/mouth downward. Right/left: yaw only the head 25-35 degrees, shifting the face center and tiny nose/mouth toward the respective screen edge; show near and far cheek foreshortening. Keep the face visible in all poses.

Intermediates combine evenly stepped pitch and yaw around the fixed body. The whole character remains upright. Every frame uses the same eye design, hair part, light, head size, outfit, lower-body anchor, and apparent scale.
